Preparation Strategy

  1. Week 1-2: Review fundamentals — data structures, Big O notation
  2. Week 3-4: Practice coding problems daily (2-3 per day)
  3. Week 5-6: Study system design patterns and practice whiteboarding
  4. Week 7-8: Mock interviews and behavioral question practice

During the Interview

  • Clarify requirements before coding
  • Think out loud — explain your reasoning
  • Start with a brute force solution, then optimize
  • Test your solution with edge cases
  • Ask thoughtful questions about the team and role
